In this update

  • EventsHalloween, Easter and Christmas events can go live for everyone. Each has its own background, bonus coins, pickups and a free event skin for players who join in.
  • Cooler backgroundsAn animated night sky behind the game, glowing fog and fireflies on every map, and a light around your reaper.
  • ChrisA tall friendly character with teal hair, a mustard hoodie and a red umbrella.
  • DanA new character in the shop. He has black hair, a black shirt that says JERK, and a baseball bat.
  • Background changerA button at the top right of the lobby lets everyone pick a lobby background, like the Headless Horseman, the Grim Reaper, a haunted forest and more. You can also send the owner a request for a new one.
  • Headless HorsemanA new rider skin in the shop. It gallops on a ghost horse with a glowing pumpkin under its arm and swings a sword. Everyone it takes loses their head, and it has three powers: Headhunt (gallop and behead everyone in the way), Pumpkin bomb and Terror (freezes everyone nearby in fear).
  • Six more eventsNew events: Faceless Forest (a foggy night forest with a free Hollow Watcher skin; matches move to the woods, a faceless Watcher stalks you, and once every soul is taken you must defeat him: 5,000 health, a max reaper hits for 1,000), Summer Beach, Valentine's, Space, Harvest and Lucky Day. Each one changes the whole game, adds coin pickups and gives a free skin while it is live. Coin bonuses stay small.
  • Disco and Toon eventsTwo new events. Disco turns the game into a dance floor with a mirror ball, lights and records to grab. Toon turns it into a cartoon with POW and BONK pop-ups and stars to grab. Each has its own free skin.
  • Fits your deviceThe game detects phone, tablet, Chromebook or PC and fits the screen. You can change it in Settings.
  • RolesYour role shows at the top right. The owner shows as Owner and everyone else starts as New. The owner can change roles.
  • Item shopThe new Shop tab sells 5 limited special skins every day. They change at 7:00 PM your time.
  • 20 special skinsPumpkin King, Angel of Mercy, Glitch Phantom, Eclipse Emperor and more. Each has its own look and story.
  • 3 new skinsMoss Warden, Ember Scorch and Desert Pharaoh.
  • Every skin is differentEach reaper has its own hood, armour and scythe, and a story about how it was made.
  • Longer scythe reachBetter skins reach farther. Neon Phantom and every special skin reach the whole map.
  • Game modesNormal, Creative with endless enemies, and Battle where you pick how many.
  • ModsBuy mods with coins, like Range Hack, Death Radar and Code Peek.
  • No timersRounds last until you take every soul.
